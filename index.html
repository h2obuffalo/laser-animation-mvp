Representation guidance

Humanoid abstraction reduces an air dancer to a body, arms, base, and head. Simplified subject outline follows the left and right silhouette edges to preserve more of the original shape. It can also work for a person or object that is clearly separated from a relatively consistent background. Keep the outline point count low for smoother laser scanning.
